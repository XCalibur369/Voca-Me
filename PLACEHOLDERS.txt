Hey! Here is a definitive list of all the images, audio, and music files the game is currently trying to load.

Drop these files directly into the `static/images/` and `static/audio/` folders in this project directory. If the game doesn't find them, it will just silently ignore them or use a fallback circle, so don't worry about it crashing if you don't have them yet!

------------------------------------------
🖼️ IMAGES (`static/images/`)
------------------------------------------
The game supports both .jpg and .png formats!

1. PLAYER PROFILES (For the Intake Form):
   - `profile1.jpg`
   - `profile2.jpg`
   - `profile3.jpg`
   - `profile4.jpg`
   - `profile5.jpg`

2. NPC AVATARS:
   (The game automatically looks for the character's exact name in lowercase)
   - `interviewer.jpg` (or `tortoise.jpg` depending on what you name them in the script!)
   - `jesse.jpg`
   - `walt.jpg`

3. BACKGROUNDS:
   - `desert_bg.jpg` (Used to set the vibe for the Breaking Bad chapter)

------------------------------------------
🔊 SOUND EFFECTS (`static/audio/`)
------------------------------------------
These play at specific moments to enhance the immersion!

- `fall_wind.mp3` (Plays when the player falls into Chapter 2)
- `splat.mp3` (Plays when they hit the asphalt)
- `desert_wind.mp3` (Ambient wind when they approach the RV)
- `rv_door.mp3` (Plays when Walt enters the RV)
- `glass_shatter.mp3` (Plays during the chemistry explosion AND when shooting asteroids in Space Blasters!)
- `success_chime.mp3` (Plays when they solve the chemistry equation correctly)

------------------------------------------
🎵 BACKGROUND MUSIC
------------------------------------------
Right now, the `bgAudio` hook is set up in `script.js` but isn't tied to any files yet. If you want a looping track, let me know the filename (e.g. `breaking_bad_theme.mp3`) and I will trigger it to play as soon as Chapter 2 starts!
